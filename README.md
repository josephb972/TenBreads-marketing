# Tenbreads

**Automated B2B Foodservice Invoice Auditor & Recovery Engine**

Tenbreads cross-references every food and beverage delivery invoice line-by-line against a restaurant group's contracted price agreements, catches distributor margin creep and uncontracted substitutions, drafts credit claim packets, and tracks credits until they hit the bank.

- **For:** multi-unit restaurant / hospitality groups (5–100+ locations, sweet spot 8–35)
- **Audits:** Sysco, US Foods, PFG, GFS, Cheney Brothers (US); Brakes, Bidfood, Reynolds, Direct Seafoods, Booker (UK)
- **Pricing:** free 90-day lookback audit → 25% contingency, $500–$1,500/mo SaaS, or hybrid (~$299/mo + 12–15%)
