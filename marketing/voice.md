# Voice

- Operator-facing, plain, dollar-concrete: cents/lb, $/case, $/drop. Say "£" for UK prospects.
- Short sentences. One ask per message. No "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "leverage", "synergy".
- Lead with the prospect's problem or a specific signal, not with us.
- Cold email: under 110 words, no attachments, no tracking-pixel language, one CTA: "Want us to run the free 90-day lookback on your [Distributor] account?"
- Subject lines: lowercase-ish, under 6 words, no clickbait, no "Re:" tricks.
- Be honest: no invented clients, awards, or guaranteed recoveries. Say "typical industry leakage is around 1.8%" only with "illustrative".
- Required in every cold email: sender name, physical address, unsubscribe line (see config.md).
