# Voice

- Operator-facing, plain, dollar-concrete: cents/lb, $/case, $/drop. Say "£" for UK prospects.
- Short sentences. One ask per message. No "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "leverage", "synergy".
- Lead with the prospect's problem or a specific signal, not with us.
- Cold email: under 90 words, plain and literal (see the Plain-language rule below), no attachments, no tracking-pixel language, one CTA: "Want us to run the free 90-day lookback on your [Distributor] account?"
- Subject lines: lowercase-ish, under 6 words, no clickbait, no "Re:" tricks.
- Be honest: no invented clients, awards, or guaranteed recoveries. Say "typical industry leakage is around 1.8%" only with "illustrative".
- Required in every cold email: sender name, physical address, unsubscribe line (see config.md).

## Plain-language rule (owner, 2026-11-21)
Emails must be simple and straightforward. No figures of speech, idioms, or flourishes.
- Banned: "drift quietly", "rarely gets noticed", "keeps growing", "pass this to", "whoever handles", "on the dock", and similar. Say the plain fact.
- Use literal words: "price", "bill", "check", "credit". One idea per sentence, about 15 words or fewer.
- No scene-setting or compliments. One sentence on why we are writing, one on what we do, one ask.
- Template (keep under 90 words):

  Hello [Name or Company] team,

  I am writing to [Name/Role]. [One factual trigger from the prospect row.]

  Tenbreads checks each supplier invoice line against your agreed prices. We find overcharges and claim the credit. Example (illustrative): agreed £3.80/kg, billed £4.15/kg.

  Can we run a free 90-day check on your [Distributor] account? It uses your own invoices and costs nothing.

  Joseph at Tenbreads
  119B Kenton Road, Harrow, HA3 0AZ, United Kingdom
  Reply STOP and I won't email you again.
