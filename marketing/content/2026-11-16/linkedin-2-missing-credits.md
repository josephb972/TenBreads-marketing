# LinkedIn post 2 - Missing promised credits (persona: CFO / VP Finance, US, $)
Status: draft. Owner publishes. Not posted.

The driver wrote "credit $86.40 - 2 cs damaged" on the delivery receipt. Six weeks later, it is not on the vendor statement.

Nobody lied. The note was handwritten, the receipt went in a folder at the site, and the credit request never made it into the distributor's system. The invoice was paid in full.

Illustrative math: 12 locations, one missed credit of about $85 per location every two weeks, is about $26,500 a year. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

The insight for finance: a promised credit is a receivable. Most groups do not book it as one. If it is not logged when it is promised, there is nothing to reconcile against when the statement arrives, so the gap is invisible.

The fix is a short list: every credit promised on the dock, with invoice number, amount and date, matched against each statement until it appears.
