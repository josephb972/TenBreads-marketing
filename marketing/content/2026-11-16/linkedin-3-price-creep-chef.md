# LinkedIn post 3 - Contract price creep (persona: Executive Chef / F&B Director, US, $)
Status: draft. Owner publishes. Not posted.

Heavy cream contracted at $28.40 a case. Billed this month at $30.10. Same product, same pack, $1.70 more a case.

Your menu has not changed. Your portions have not changed. Your food cost percentage still moved, and someone will ask the kitchen why.

Illustrative math: 8 cases a week per location across 12 locations at $1.70 over is $163 a week, or about $8,500 a year, on one dairy line. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

The insight: when food cost drifts and the recipes are tight, check the unit price on the invoice before you check the line cooks. Creep moves a few cents at a time, so it reads as "the market" rather than a contract miss.

One habit helps: pick your ten highest-volume items and compare the billed unit price to the contracted price every week, not at renewal.
