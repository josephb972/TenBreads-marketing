# LinkedIn post 3 - Catch-weight discrepancies (persona: AP / Finance Manager)
Status: draft. Owner publishes. Not posted.

Salmon at $9.50/lb. Net weight received: 40 lb. Weight billed: 42 lb. That is $19.00 on one case.

You have 150 invoices to get through this week. Nobody is re-weighing fish.

Illustrative math: 25 catch-weight cases a week across a 12-unit group, each 2 lb heavy on the invoice, is $475 a week, or about $24,700 a year. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

Meat, seafood and cheese are billed by actual weight. Price per pound should apply to net weight, not gross weight with ice, liquid and packaging, and not a nominal case weight. When the invoice uses the wrong one, the line still looks normal.

What helps AP: ask receiving to record net weight on the delivery note for catch-weight items, then compare billed pounds to received pounds before approving.
