Your supply agreement says "all-in delivered pricing". The invoice says £38 drop charge.

Illustrative math: 12 sites, 3 deliveries a site each week, £38 a drop. That is 36 drops and £1,368 a week, or about £71,000 a year, for a charge your agreement may already cover. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

Drop fees, fuel adders, pallet charges and split-case fees rarely arrive as one line you would notice. They show up as small additions, site by site, week by week. Each one is too small for anyone to query on its own.

With restaurant net margins of 3-7%, a few thousand pounds a month of unagreed charges is not rounding. It is profit.

The check is simple to describe: list the adders your agreement allows, then flag every invoice line that is not on the list.
