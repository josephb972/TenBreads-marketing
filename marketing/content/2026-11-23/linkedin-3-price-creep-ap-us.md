# LinkedIn post 3 - Contract price creep (persona: AP / Finance Manager, US, $)
Status: draft. Owner publishes. Not posted.

Chicken breast contracted at $3.80/lb. Billed at $3.94 in March. Billed at $4.15 by June.

Each step is only cents a pound. Each invoice looks close to the last one, so it passes.

Illustrative math: 1,200 lb a week across the group, $0.35/lb over the contract price. That is $420 a week, about $21,800 a year, on one item. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

The insight for AP: comparing an invoice to last week's invoice will not find this. The only comparison that works is invoice price against the contract price, for every line. At 150 invoices a week, that is not possible by hand.

If you cannot check every line, check the highest-spend items first. Chicken, beef, dairy and oil are usually the largest lines.
