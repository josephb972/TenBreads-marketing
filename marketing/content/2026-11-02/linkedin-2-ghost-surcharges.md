# LinkedIn post 2 - Ghost surcharges (persona: Ops Director / Head of Supply Chain)
Status: draft. Owner publishes. Not posted.

A $45 drop fee on a contract that says "all-in delivered pricing".

Illustrative math: 12 locations, 2 deliveries a week each, $45 per drop. That is $1,080 a week, or about $56,160 a year, for a charge your price master says should not exist. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

A common pattern: fuel adders, pallet fees and split-case fees get added at the invoice level, not the price file. The rep never "changed your price", so nobody flags it. It just shows up as a line near the bottom.

If you signed the price master, you are the person who knows what "all-in" was meant to cover. The invoice should say the same thing.

Quick check you can do today: pull last month's invoices from one distributor and filter for any line that is not a product. Compare it against the freight and fuel terms in your agreement.
