# LinkedIn post 1 - Price creep (persona: CFO / VP Finance)
Status: draft. Owner publishes. Not posted.

Chicken breast contracted at $3.80/lb, billed at $4.15/lb. That is 35 cents a pound, and nobody signed off on it.

Illustrative math: a 12-unit group buying 400 lb a week per location is 4,800 lb. At 35 cents over, that is $1,680 a week, or about $87,360 a year. On one protein. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

Creep rarely arrives as one big jump. It goes $3.80, then $3.94, then $4.15. Each step looks too small for AP to chase, and the contract price sits in a PDF nobody opens at invoice time.

With net margins of 3-7% in most restaurant groups, every recovered dollar drops straight to the bottom line.

The fix is dull: match every invoice line to the contracted price, every week, not once a year at renewal.
