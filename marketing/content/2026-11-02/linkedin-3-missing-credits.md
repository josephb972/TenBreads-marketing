The driver wrote a £212 credit on the delivery note. It never showed up on the statement.

Illustrative math: four credits like that a month across a group is £848 a month, or about £10,176 a year, owed to you and not applied. (Illustrative numbers, not a client result.)

It is not anyone's fault inside your team. Delivery notes get signed in the kitchen, photographed or filed, and the monthly statement arrives weeks later. Matching one against the other by hand, across 150+ documents a week, is a job that always loses to the urgent work.

What helps: log every promised credit the day it is written, then check it against the next statement. If it is not there, raise the claim with the delivery note reference and invoice number attached.
