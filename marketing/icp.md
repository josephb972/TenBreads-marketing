# Ideal customer profile and offer

## Product
Tenbreads: automated B2B foodservice invoice auditor and recovery engine. Checks every distributor invoice line by line against the contracted price agreement, catches overcharges, drafts credit claim packets, and tracks credits until they hit the bank.

## Target
- Multi-unit hospitality operators, restaurant groups, franchise collectives, gastropub operators, boutique hotel F&B groups.
- 5–100+ locations; **sweet spot 8–35 units**.
- Exclude: 1-unit independents (too little volume, no master contract); 1,000+ unit chains (in-house AP audit).
- Distributors: US — Sysco, US Foods, PFG, GFS, Cheney Brothers. UK — Brakes, Bidfood, Reynolds, Direct Seafoods, Booker.

## Personas
| Persona | Angle |
|---|---|
| CFO / VP Finance | Net margins are 3–7%; recovered overcharges drop straight to the bottom line |
| Ops Director / Head of Supply Chain | Signs the price master; hates reps quietly moving margins |
| Executive Chef / F&B Director | Approves dock substitutions without seeing the billed markup |
| AP / Finance Manager | 150+ invoices a week, no time to verify line pricing |

## Five problems (content pillars)
1. Contract price creep — e.g. chicken breast $3.80/lb → $3.94 → $4.15
2. Unauthorized SKU substitution — $41.80/cs billed vs $32.50 contracted
3. Ghost surcharges and split-case adders — e.g. $45/drop despite "all-in delivered pricing"
4. Catch-weight discrepancies — gross container weight billed instead of net
5. Missing promised credits — driver's handwritten credit memo never hits the statement

## Offer ladder
- **Hook: free 90-day lookback audit** on one distributor account. Shows real recoverable dollars on the prospect's own invoices.
- Contingency: 25% of credits actually booked; $0 out of pocket.
- SaaS: $500/mo (5–15 units), $950/mo (16–40), custom 40+.
- Hybrid (recommended): about $299/mo + 12–15% contingency.

## Illustrative example (always label as illustrative, never as a client result)
12-unit group, Sysco account: about $4.68M annual purchasing, 1.8% average leakage = about $84,240 a year, vs $6,000/yr at $500/mo.

## Operator one-liner
"You pay 150 distributor invoices a week across 5–100 locations. We catch the $0.18/lb price drift, substituted SKUs, and bogus drop fees your AP team doesn't have 40 hours a week to find — and we get your money back."
