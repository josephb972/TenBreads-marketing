# Tenbreads pitch deck outline

Audience: CFO / VP Finance first, then Ops Director, Executive Chef / F&B Director, and AP Manager.
Length: 10 slides, about 12 minutes plus questions.
Currency: show $, £ or €, matching the prospect's market. UK decks use £ and the UK distributors (Brakes, Bidfood, Reynolds, Direct Seafoods, Booker).

Every dollar figure about a 12-unit group is labelled **illustrative example**. Never present it as a client result. Do not add logos, client names, testimonials or awards that we do not have.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

- **Tenbreads**, with the TB monogram
- Headline: *Automated invoice audit and recovery for multi-unit foodservice*
- Subline: *We check every distributor invoice against your contract and claim back the overcharges.*

**Speaker note:** One sentence on who we are. Then move straight to the problem.

---

## Slide 2 — The problem

- You pay 150 distributor invoices a week across 5–100 locations.
- Your AP team checks totals, not line prices.
- Overcharges are small per line, so they are easy to miss and add up over a year.

**Speaker note:** Ask the room: "Who checks every line price on every invoice today?" Pause for the answer.

---

## Slide 3 — Five ways distributors bill above contract

| # | What happens | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contract price creep | Chicken breast agreed at $3.80/lb, billed at $3.94, then $4.15 |
| 2 | Unauthorized substitution | Out-of-stock item swapped, billed at $41.80/cs vs $32.50 contracted |
| 3 | Ghost surcharges and split-case adders | $45/drop fee despite "all-in delivered pricing" |
| 4 | Catch-weight errors | Gross container weight billed instead of net |
| 5 | Missing promised credits | Driver's handwritten credit never appears on the statement |

**Speaker note:** Use the one line that matches the room. CFOs respond to #1. Chefs respond to #2.

---

## Slide 4 — What Tenbreads does

Four steps:

1. **Load your contract.** Upload the price schedule once per renewal.
2. **Receive invoices.** Email forwarding, PDF upload, or folder sync.
3. **Match every line.** Compare item code, pack size, unit of measure, delivered weight and unit rate against your contract.
4. **Claim the credit.** We draft a dispute packet citing your contract clause, the invoice number and the amount owed.

**Speaker note:** Plain steps only. No diagram needed for the first pass.

## Slide 8 — How you pay

| Option | Model | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Contingency | 25% of credits actually booked. $0 out of pocket. | Operators who want no fixed cost |
| SaaS | $500/mo (5–15 units), $950/mo (16–40 units), custom 40+ | Operators with steady invoice volume |
| Hybrid (recommended) | About $299/mo plus 12–15% of recovered cash | Most groups |

Example (illustrative): $10,000 found under Contingency. You keep $7,500. Tenbreads earns $2,500.

**Speaker note:** For the SaaS option, show the break-even: at $15k/mo of overcharges, 25% is $3,750/mo against a $500 flat fee. Check the figures before presenting.

---

## Slide 9 — Who it is for

- **Good fit:** 8–35 units (sweet spot), 5–100 locations in total, a master purchasing contract with at least one distributor.
- **Not a fit:** single-unit independents (too little volume, no master contract). Chains with 1,000+ units (they run in-house audit teams and custom EDI).
- **Distributors we audit:** US: Sysco, US Foods, PFG, GFS, Cheney Brothers. UK: Brakes, Bidfood, Reynolds, Direct Seafoods, Booker.

**Speaker note:** Ask which distributors they use today. Do not claim we already audit any specific account.
