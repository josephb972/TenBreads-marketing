Your distributor invoices are drifting off contract. We find it and get the money back.

You pay 150 distributor invoices a week across 5–100 locations. We catch the $0.18/lb price drift, substituted SKUs, and bogus drop fees your AP team doesn't have 40 hours a week to find.

What we catch

Contract price creep

Chicken breast contracted at $3.80/lb drifts to $3.94, then $4.15.

Unauthorized substitutions

Out-of-stock item swapped and billed at $41.80/cs against $32.50 contracted.

Ghost surcharges

$45/drop fuel or drop fees despite "all-in delivered pricing"; split-case and pallet adders.

Catch-weight errors

Meat, seafood and cheese billed at gross container weight instead of net delivered weight.

Missing credits

The driver's handwritten credit memo that never reaches the monthly statement.

How it works

  1. Upload your contract price schedule once. Invoices arrive by email forwarding, PDF upload, or EDI/folder sync.
  2. 3-way line-by-line match. Item codes, pack sizes, UOM, delivered weights and billed rates against contract rates, substitution rules and approved adders.
  3. Dispute claim packet. Exact variance per line (e.g. billed $12.50/lb vs contract $10.84/lb = $74.70), plus a formal claim memo citing the contract clause and invoice number.
  4. Credit tracking. Drafted → Dispatched → Vendor Acknowledged → Credit Note Issued & Reconciled.
