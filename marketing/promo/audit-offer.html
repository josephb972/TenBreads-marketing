What we look for

Price drift

Chicken breast contracted at $3.80/lb, billed at $3.94, then $4.15.

Substitutions

Out-of-stock swap billed at $41.80/cs against $32.50 contracted.

Ghost surcharges

$45/drop fees and split-case adders despite "all-in delivered pricing".

Catch-weight errors

Gross container weight billed instead of net delivered weight.

Missing credits

The driver's handwritten credit memo that never reaches the statement.

What you get

  1. A line-by-line audit report for one distributor account, covering 90 days of invoices.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The 90-day lookback on one distributor account costs nothing and has no obligation. You see recoverable dollars on your own invoices before choosing a pricing option.

What if you find nothing?

Then we tell you so. We don't promise a recovery; the lookback measures what is actually there.

How does contingency work?

25% of credits the distributor actually books. If nothing is credited, you owe nothing. Your card is authorized on file via Stripe when a claim packet is generated, and the fee is finalized only when the distributor issues the credit.
